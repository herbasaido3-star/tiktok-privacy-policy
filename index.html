Privacy, Terms & Public Multi-User Use Case

At Saidos Workflow, data protection, transparency, and global accessibility are our highest priorities. This application is a public multi-user SaaS platform designed to help independent creators, business entities, and marketing agencies worldwide manage and optimize their TikTok presence efficiently and securely.

1. Public SaaS & Commercial Distribution

Important Note for Reviewers: Saidos Workflow is NOT an internal company tool, nor is it limited to personal use. It functions as a public automation integration bridge available to any registered TikTok user or independent third-party creator. Users log in individually using TikTok's official Login Kit to sync their own, self-managed data. We do not display posts from our own managed accounts on this landing page; instead, we offer a dynamic infrastructure for the global creator community.

2. Secure OAuth Data Separation

Every user who connects their TikTok profile to Saidos Workflow via secure OAuth environment retains full control. Data isolation is strictly maintained, ensuring that profile statistics and video metadata are only visible and accessible to the authorized creator who initiated the connection.
