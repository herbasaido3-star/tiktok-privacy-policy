Privacy Policy for Saidos Workflow

We are very delighted that you have shown interest in our enterprise. Data protection is of a particularly high priority for the management of Saidos Workflow. The use of the Internet pages of Saidos Workflow is possible without any indication of personal data; however, if a data subject wants to use special enterprise services via our website, processing of personal data could become necessary. If the processing of personal data is necessary and there is no statutory basis for such processing, we generally obtain consent from the data subject.

The processing of personal data, such as the name, address, e-mail address, or telephone number of a data subject shall always be in line with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and in accordance with the country-specific data protection regulations applicable to Saidos Workflow.

1. Definitions

The data protection declaration of Saidos Workflow is based on the terms used by the European legislator for the adoption of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

  • a) Personal data: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • b) Data subject: Any identified or identifiable natural person whose data is processed.
  • c) Processing: Any operation performed on personal data.
  • d) Restriction of processing: Marking stored data to limit future processing.
  • e) Profiling: Automated processing to evaluate personal aspects.
  • f) Pseudonymisation: Processing data so it can't be attributed without additional info.
  • g) Controller: The body which determines the purposes and means of processing.
  • h) Processor: Natural or legal person processing data on behalf of the controller.
  • i) Recipient: Entity to which data is disclosed.
  • j) Third party: Any body other than those listed above.
  • k) Consent: Freely given, specific indication of the data subject's wishes.
